- **Retailer** (`seller`)

This document is used solely for the purpose of verifying the legitimacy and legal status of the supplier's business. Uploaded verification documents are retained for compliance and fraud-prevention purposes only.

### Supplier Account Approval Authority

Suppliers have the capability to approve retailer account creation requests when the retailer's account creation form references the supplier's company in the "referred by" or equivalent field. Suppliers must exercise this approval authority responsibly and in accordance with the following:

- Suppliers are responsible for verifying that they recognize and have a legitimate business relationship with the retailer before approving the account.
- Suppliers are strongly advised not to approve account creation requests from retailers or users they do not recognize or have not engaged with.
- Suppliers must not approve accounts for the purpose of enabling fraud, unauthorized sales, misuse of company identity, or other unlawful activity.
- Approval of accounts by suppliers implies the supplier's confirmation that the retailer is a legitimate business entity and that approval is made in good faith.
